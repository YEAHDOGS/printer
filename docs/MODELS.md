# DOGS printer lineup — product goals

Thesis: printers that work for the people, not the other way around.
No tracking watermarks, no spyware, no ink-by-the-thimble, no driver hell.

The incumbents had thirty years and spent them making printers dumber.
We're done waiting.

## Models

### 1. DOGS Printer (base) — in development

The reinvention: no tracking dots, no phone-home, gallon-jug ink tanks,
subscriptions never bound to the hardware, high-DPI output at high speed,
driverless native printing on every device, silent models, custom colors,
instant startup.

### 2. DOGS Poster (advanced) — goal, ~$1,000 target price

Wide-format, state-of-the-art. Full-size wall posters: feed it large paper,
hang prints big enough to cover most of a door. Prints on cardboard.
The poster market as we know it ends here.

### 3. DOGS Labels — goal

The bottle label printer. Design it, print it, stick it on the bottle.

### 4. DOGS Stickers — goal

The sticker printer. Stickers on demand, any shape, any size. Peel and stick.

### 5. DOGS 3D — in the lab

Long-term research. The technology has a long way to go; we're going anyway.

## Stage honesty

Nothing above ships until it works. Base printer first, Poster second,
Labels and Stickers after, 3D when the tech earns it. The site shows the
lineup we're building toward — not a store.
